# SPARK — challenge alignment checklist

| Challenge requirement | Where it is addressed | Honest status |
|---|---|---|
| Understand final-year engineering students | Growth plan and student-first project preview | Audience/friction are hypotheses to validate, not research findings |
| Plan for 500 registrations in 7 days with ₹2,000 | Five-slide growth plan and seven-day playbook | Targets and funnel rates are assumptions, not a forecast or results |
| Choose a few channels and explain why | Student ambassadors, clubs/class reps, permission-based WhatsApp/organic | Simulation only; no outreach performed |
| Build one working asset | Student-facing Project Matchmaker + demo registration + growth workspace | Local prototype with FastAPI + SQLite |
| Track registration and conversion | Tagged-link builder, funnel ledger, observed-only summary, CSV export | Tracking links need real analytics capture; event figures must be entered honestly |
| Learn and iterate | A/B hypothesis log with a conservative decision rule | No winner should be claimed before a real, comparable test |
| Explain AI learning and rejected suggestions | AI learning notes and video script | Edit the examples so they accurately reflect the prompts and decisions actually made |
| Three-minute video | Script provided separately | Record your own walkthrough; do not claim the campaign ran |

## Submission guardrails
- Keep the growth plan within five slides / two pages.
- Include the prototype link or demo and a three-minute video.
- Explain what changed from the first idea, what another 24 hours would improve, and one AI suggestion rejected with a reason.
- Never present demo database rows, target counts, or assumed conversion rates as actual campaign results.
